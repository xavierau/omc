## Key decisions
- "Schedule off" for staff means hours stored with the switch off; a restaurant with no hours at all still shows no block (unchanged for every tenant today).
- Limits and stops stay stored while off and come back when switched on (walk step 13).
- A stopped slot reads 已滿 (brief); the W2 mockup showed a stopped slot without a badge. Its stopped line and switch are unchanged.
- The period 已滿 badge shows whenever the period is full (meal reached, or every slot full), not when it is stopped.
- Copy: "Meal full / 餐已滿"; "Booking hours are off, so guest limits and stops don't apply." / "訂座時段已關閉，人數上限及停止訂座不適用。"
